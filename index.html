About

Embodied AI, from hardware to policy

I'm an MEng Robotics and AI student at University College London (Computer Science & Engineering). I work across the full embodied-AI stack in C++ and Python — ROS 2 systems, perception and state estimation, real-time control and locomotion, and learning-based decision making with reinforcement learning, diffusion models and LLM agents.

As a member of the UCL Humanoid Lab I help develop a 22-DOF humanoid robot. In 2026 I built the perception and MAPPO decision layers of a multi-robot pursuit system trialled on a Unitree Go2, proposed a braked-wheel “skating” quadruped that uses ~21% less energy per metre and moves 1.44× faster, and ran a 794-run study on how multimodal robot policies should be evaluated.

2026 · MuJoCo · Isaac Lab / PhysX · 6 robots

Skating Quadruped: Passive Braked Wheels for Efficient Locomotion

My own locomotion idea: fit a quadruped with passive, brakeable wheels and let it glide, brake and step. Across ~310,000 simulated trials on six robots and 11+ terrains it used ~21% less energy per metre (median 35%) and moved 1.44× faster than walking — confirmed in both MuJoCo and PhysX.
