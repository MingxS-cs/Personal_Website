Global Pulse — News Map & Markets Mini-Program

A WeChat mini-program that maps market-moving news by country and impact, streams ~95 global instruments with technical charts, and adds a tool-calling LLM analyst that remembers and scores its past calls.

A WeChat mini-program for following global markets in one place: a world map of market-moving news, live quotes for around 95 instruments, and an AI analyst that explains moves and keeps itself honest by scoring its own past calls.

Highlights

  • World news map on Canvas 2D (Miller projection, 180 countries), colouring each country by S / A / B / C impact level, with pop-ups for urgent items.
  • ~95 instruments — global indices, futures, FX, crypto, A-share and US sectors — with K-line charts and MA, BOLL, MACD, KDJ, RSI, WR and BIAS indicators.
  • News aggregated from a dozen Chinese and international sources, with cross-source near-duplicate removal (bigram Jaccard ≥ 0.5 plus number / direction guards: 240 → 205 items).
  • AI analyst: a tool-calling LLM agent that looks at technicals, news, fund flow and fundamentals over short / mid / long horizons, remembers its past predictions and compares them with what happened; plus an auto-generated daily market briefing.
