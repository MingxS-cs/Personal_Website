Multi‑Robot Cooperative Pursuit Simulation

2026 · Isaac Lab · Auction Algorithm · Multi‑Agent Reinforcement Learning

Built a simulation in Isaac Lab in which a team of Unitree quadrupeds must locate, track, encircle and intercept moving targets in complex terrain. The system pairs an Auction Algorithm for task allocation with multi‑agent reinforcement learning for cooperative behaviour, so that pursuers dynamically split roles, share observations and converge on the target without explicit central control.

Highlights

  • Multi‑agent Isaac Lab environment with Unitree quadruped models, randomised terrain and adversarial targets.
  • Auction Algorithm assigns roles (pursuer / blocker / scout) and re‑allocates dynamically as the situation changes.
  • Multi‑agent RL policies trained for cooperative encirclement and interception under partial observability.
  • Designed for transfer toward real Unitree platforms, with locomotion controllers separated from high‑level planning.
